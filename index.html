About the Book

She was sent undercover to a tiny Tasmanian town to buy a family oyster farm. He saw through her on day one. Now she's caught between the career she's been chasing and the life she never saw coming — and a town that's not going to make this easy.

  • Grumpy-sunshine romance
  • Forced proximity in a small coastal town
  • Undercover identity / secret she can't keep
  • Fish out of water heroine
  • A meddling town that's already planning the wedding
  • Steamy slow burn with a hero who's worth the wait

About Zoey

ZB

Zoey Bell writes sharp, swoony romantic comedies about women who find exactly what they weren't looking for. Her debut novel, A Hard Shell to Crack, was inspired by a love of small coastal towns, grumpy heroes, and the chaos of being an outsider in a place that decides to keep you.

When she's not writing, you can find her overthinking her coffee order, arguing with her TBR pile, and plotting her next book.
